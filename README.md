## The Problem: AI is Training on Stolen Data
The numbers are shocking: over 5.8 billion images, 300 million code repositories, and 50 million songs are currently being scraped from the internet without consent[cite: 6]. This massive violation of intellectual property has resulted in over $1.5 billion in damages sought via lawsuits[cite: 6]. 

The industry is missing critical institutional governance on tracking used data, compensating creators, and auditing models for IP infringement[cite: 6]. This hackathon focuses on resolving this exact ethical gap, paired with one of the most critical challenges in AI today: enabling agents to operate with verifiable trust and real financial capability. 

## The Solution: ALL & AI
Our response translated into a marketplace for everything: one platform to buy and sell music, code, 3D models, animations, and any creative output. This is how **ALL & AI** came about.

We built a system where AI agents can safely interact with capital, execute strategies, and demonstrate transparent behavior. This is a Multi-Asset Ecosystem Powered by Nanopayments and Ethical AI, where each component plays a role central to the project's scope:

*   **Nanopayments for Micro-Sales:** Traditional platforms lock out "micro-creators" due to high transaction fees[cite: 6]. Our DeFi nanopayments allow users to sell small assets for cents, making "micro-purchases" ($0.10 - $2.00) feasible and unlocking the creator middle class[cite: 6].
*   **Transparent AI Generating:** Users can generate new, highly customizable variations of art or products based on reference pieces[cite: 6]. The AI agent automatically tracks the source material and executes smart contracts to pay the original artists their fair share[cite: 6].
*   **Consented Data Licensing:** Artists decide if they want to sell their work to AI companies for model training[cite: 6]. By acting as a transparent intermediary, we tap into a $3.8 Billion B2B market, providing AI developers with the explicitly consented, "clean" datasets they desperately need[cite: 6].

## Revenue Model & Ecosystem Mapping
Our intelligent agents govern a transparent revenue stream based on:
1.  **Transaction fees:** A small cut from every direct sale on the marketplace[cite: 6].
2.  **AI Generation fees:** Split equitably between our platform and the original artists tracked by the agent[cite: 6].
3.  **B2B Data Sales:** Selling ethically sourced, consented data to enterprise LLM developers[cite: 6].
